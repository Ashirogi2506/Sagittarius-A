# Moon playable slice

The `MoonEnvironment.fbx` environment is an experimental export of `FarSignal-Moon-70.blend`. It preserves evaluated collection instances, including Beacon pad, cart and Pole-7; the source was never modified. `Tools/Blender/export_moon_environment.py` documents the export. The available Blender 4.5 reports that the source was written by a newer version, so artwork and materials require visual comparison with the authoring Blender version before being approved.

In the Unity editor, open `Assets/Scenes/GameplayUI.unity`, enter Play mode and choose **MOON SURFACE TEST** from the main menu. This editor-only shortcut uses an isolated in-memory handoff and does not modify the campaign save. In the intended campaign flow, the arrival screen's **LAND ON MOON** uses the actual Earth handoff. The cargo, power and landing minigame runners are still required to reach it normally.

Controls: WASD movement, mouse look, E to interact (hold E on dust), Escape unlocks the cursor, click to relock, Tab returns to the UI. Press E to confirm the saved landing plan, then follow the objective and orange interaction markers. The cart sequence is start, power off, open cover, clean three patches, close cover, retest, then load panel and kit.

`MoonPlayableExperience` builds the current surface around the imported model at runtime, while `MoonFirstPersonController`, `MoonWorldTarget`, `MoonSceneConnector` and `MoonStageController` separate movement, interaction, scene events and gameplay state. This is a first playable slice, not the full Moon chapter. Cargo loading visuals, sound, save checkpoints beyond the local draft, player control rebinding, failed-descent checkpoint restoration, Moon objectives after the cart and final scene loading remain team integration work. The target markers and prompts are temporary presentation; the gameplay contract is intended to remain reusable.

Before treating the slice as verified, test in Unity: import the FBX without errors; check terrain and cart scale, camera height, colliders, prompts and marker visibility; complete all interactions in order; stop/restart the test; and test campaign handoff plus save/resume once the teammate minigame runners are present. Verify both Beacon and Ridge landing outcomes with the campaign team. Ridge-specific spawning is not implemented, so the arrival screen disables surface entry for Ridge and preserves its landing plan.
